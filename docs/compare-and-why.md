# Standard vs custom metrics

**Standard (context):** spend, impressions, CPM, link CTR, cost per purchase, Meta ROAS. They tell you how the ad was delivered and what Meta credited.

**Custom (ranking creative):** seconds viewed per impression, thumbstop rate, hook rate, CTA strength, first-party orders and revenue per 1,000 impressions, new-customer share, Meta-to-first-party ratio, comments per 1,000 impressions, days since post created.

Why per impression? So a heavier-spend ad doesn't win just by running more.

Why not cost per view or cost per click? Both move with CPMs and the auction, so you can't tell how much of a change is people engaging and how much is price.

Show Meta's credit beside first-party orders. Don't pick a winner. The gap is the finding.
